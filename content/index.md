Hi, I'm **Apoorva Sahay**. My journey has taken me from Patna to SASTRA, then Bangalore, across to Dallas, and now to Austin.

I'm starting this blog for two simple reasons: to keep up with fast-moving tech and to challenge myself. I'm lazy by nature — left alone, I'll read a few pages, nod along, and forget half of it by tomorrow. The goal here is to build a real habit: learn one thing at a time, talk through it in my own words, and document it in public.
